## Materials
- **Precursor Materials**:
  - Cerium nitrate hexahydrate (Ce(NO3)3·6H2O): 2 g.
  - Sodium hydroxide: 0.4 M solution.
  
- **Solvents**:
  - Milli-Q water.
  - Ethanol absolute (1:4 v/v with water for chemical precipitation, 4:1 v/v for hydrothermal method).
  
- **Reagents**:
  - Nitric acid (HNO3).
  - 1 M NaOH solution for adjusting pH.

- **Specifications**:
  - All chemicals of analytical grade from Merck, Darmstadt, Germany.

## Synthesis Equipment

- **Centrifuge**: Used at 4000 rpm for 15 minutes.

- **Drying Oven**: Temperature range of 50–70 °C for 12 hours.
  
- **Teflon-lined Stainless Steel Autoclave**: Used for hydrothermal synthesis at 150 °C for 12 hours.

## Synthesis Procedure

### Wet Chemical Precipitation

1. Dissolve 2 g of Ce(NO3)3·6H2O in a mixture of Milli-Q water and ethanol (1:4 v/v).
2. Add the solution drop-wise to a 0.4 M sodium hydroxide solution under continuous stirring, maintaining pH ~11.
3. Stir until a yellow suspension forms (~30 min). 
4. Wash the precipitate with de-ionized water and ethanol; centrifuge and dry at 50–70 °C for 12 h. 
5. Denote the sample as CP (Chemical Precipitation).

### Hydrothermal Modification

1. Transfer the precipitated NPs into a Teflon-lined stainless-steel autoclave.
2. Seal and maintain at 150 °C for 12 hours.
3. Wash, centrifuge, and dry the precipitate at 70 °C for 12 h.
4. Denote the sample as HT (Hydrothermal Treatment).

## Characterization Methods and Equipment

- **X-ray Powder Diffraction (XRD)**: D8 ADVANCE diffractometer (Bruker AXS) with Cu-Kα radiation. 2θ = 10–80° at room temperature.
  
- **BET Surface Area Measurement**: Gemini VII, ASAP 2020 (Micromeritics Instrument) using nitrogen sorption isotherms at −195 °C after a 12 h degas at 110 °C.
  
- **Zeta Potential Measurements**: Zeta-sizer (Nano ZS, Malvern, UK).

- **Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscopy (FE-SEM)**: JSM-7100F (JEOL).

## Product Characteristics

- **Final Product**: CeO2 nanoparticles.
- **Sorption Capacities**: 
  - HT-1: 192 ± 10 mg Mo·g^−1
  - CP-2: 184 ± 12 mg Mo·g^−1
- **Morphological Characteristics**: Mesoporous structure with a spherical morphology in hydrothermally treated samples and a rough morphology in chemically precipitated samples.
